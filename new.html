          First time

          1. Open the Add tab and tap your name. If it isn't there, type it once.
          2. Tick Push-ups, Sit-ups, or both, then tap Join.
          3. Your phone remembers you. You won't need to enter your name again.

          Adding reps

          1. Pick Push-ups or Sit-ups at the top, whichever you just did.
          2. Tap +5, +10, +20, or +25 after a set. For other amounts, use the + and − buttons and tap Add.
          3. Tapped by mistake? Tap Undo in the message that pops up. You have 8 seconds.
          4. Added too many earlier? Open the Me tab, find it under Your recent adds, and tap Remove.

          Your goal

          Your goal is 2,000 push-ups, 2,000 sit-ups, or both (4,000 total) for the month. You choose. Push-ups and sit-ups add up together as one count.

          The Me tab shows the daily and weekly amount that finishes your goal on time. Tap any name on the Team tab to see theirs.

          Team goal

          • There is one team total, push-ups and sit-ups together.
          • The team is at 100% only when everyone is at 100% of their own goal.
          • Extra reps past your goal are a bonus. They show on your own total but don't count toward the team.
          • Each person who joins adds their goal to the team goal.
          • The tick on each bar marks an even pace. Behind it? A small push each day catches you up.

          If someone drops out

          Tap their name on the Team tab, then tap Mark as out twice. They stop counting toward the team goal, and the goal shrinks by their share. Tap Bring back if they return. A name added by mistake with no reps can be removed there too.

          Adding the other exercise later

          Switch to it at the top of the Add tab and tap Join. Your goal goes up by 2,000.

          Cheer someone on

          Tap a name on the Team tab, then tap Send (name) a cheer. Pick a quick message or write your own.

          They see it on their Add tab and a pop-up on the TV. Tap Got it to clear it. Tap More details on any screen for the extra numbers.

          Put it on your home screen (iPhone)

          1. Open this page in Safari.
          2. Tap the Share button.
          3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

          TV board

          The TV board shows the team total, the leaderboard, and a QR code to join. Open tv.html on the TV's browser, or tap the TV board link below.
